Word-level positioned text

Every word (and every space between words) below is its own absolutely-positioned, unroled <span> — no <p>, no wrapping paragraph at all. This mimics a PDF-derived or OCR-exported fixed-layout page, where each word carries its own precomputed x/y coordinates. Positions here are computed at load time by measuring each word's real width, then laid out left to right, wrapping lines as needed — the same idea a real generator would use, just done in the browser instead of at authoring time.

Some fixed-layout EPUB exports position every single word as its own absolutely-positioned span, mimicking a PDF text layer. Reconstruction must still read this as ordinary flowing prose — e.g. an abbreviation like Mr. Kratzenstein should not break mid-sentence just because the words around it are scattered across dozens of separate elements.
